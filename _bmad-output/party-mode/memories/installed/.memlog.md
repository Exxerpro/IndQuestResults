- (dynamic) Abel convened the team to evaluate C# 15 / .NET 11 union types and closed hierarchies for IndQuestResults (v1.7.0, multi-targeting net10.0;net11.0). Winston and Amelia pushed back hard on breaking the 3-state Result<T> (Success, WithWarnings, Failure) and value-carrying failures into a binary union, while eyeing closed hierarchies for typed Error models and opt-in union bridges.
- (outcome) Abel closed the due-diligence party with a clear opt-out decision (ADR-001, GitHub Issue #7): C# 15 naive unions box value types via IUnion.Value (object?), non-boxing [Union] structs require ugly TryGetValue ceremony and collide on generic T, and unsealing Result<T> violates IndQuestResults' strictly-additive promise to consumers.
