Date: 2026-11-15 • Host: Abel • BMAD Party Mode v6.12.4

1. Naive Union Boxing

Standard public union lowers to IUnion with object? Value, silently boxing every value-type T on the heap.

2. Non-Boxing Ceremony

Custom [Union] structs require verbose TryGetValue(out ...) methods, manual tags, and collide when generic T matches error types.

3. Additive-Only Promise

Unsealing Result<T> for closed hierarchies breaks binary/JSON compatibility and violates our strictly additive contract.

4. Rich Product State

Result<T> carries Warnings, Confidence, and failure fallback values that do not fit a strict binary sum type.
